## 落地文件

- `pawcoach/events/align.py` —— `align(pose, audio, rewards, meta) -> (trials, unpaired)`
  - 每个 cue 后找最近 sit 组一轮；`latency = sit_start − cue_t`、`hold = sit_end − sit_start`
  - `reward_delay = reward_t − (marker_t 或 sit 达成时刻)`
  - `ended_by`：sit 段内有 release → release；其后紧邻 stand → stand_up；否则 unknown
- `tests/test_align.py` —— 两用例覆盖「正常配对」与「cue 无 sit」分支
